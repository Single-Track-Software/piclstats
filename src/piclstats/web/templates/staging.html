Staging

Rank a category by speed (z-score vs the field) to set start order — division by division, fastest at the front, cut into groups of 28 (four rows of 7) with a colour per group, the way the league's row sheets do it. Riders with a race this season rank on it; riders without one yet follow on last season's average (last year's 8th graders appear in JV3); riders with neither go to the back unrated.

A wave is the set of divisions that roll off the line together; its divisions share one run of group numbers and colours (Red, Yellow, Green, Blue, Orange, Neon Pink, Purple, White), restarting at group 1 for the next wave. A conference race runs Varsity + JV1 boys as one wave and JV2 + JV3 as another; every other category is one wave. A new division always opens a new group. Tick the boxes to match any other race.
