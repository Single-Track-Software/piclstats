Terms of Use

Last updated October 7, 2026 · questions: support@piclstats.com

The short version

PICL Stats is free to read for riders, families, coaches and fans. Don't scrape it, don't copy its data in bulk, and don't use it to build other datasets, products or AI models. If you'd like to use the data for something, ask first at support@piclstats.com.

Who runs this site

PICL Stats is an independent project by Single Track Software. It is not run by, or affiliated with, the Pennsylvania Interscholastic Cycling League, PAMTB or NICA. By using the site you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use it.

What you may do

Browse the site, look up riders, teams, races and courses, and share links to its pages. You may quote a few results or statistics, for example in a team newsletter or a social media post, as long as you mention PICL Stats.

What you may not do

  • Automated collection. No scraping, crawling, spidering, bulk downloading or other automated access to the site's pages or data, whether by bots, scripts, browser automation or rotating addresses. The one exception is search engines that index the site and follow its robots.txt.
  • Building datasets or models. No copying of the site's data, in whole or in large part, into another database, website, product or service, and no use of it to train or evaluate machine-learning or AI models.
  • Getting around protections. No attempts to bypass the site's security, rate limits, bot protection or sign-in, and no access to areas you haven't been invited to.
  • Misuse of rider information. Many riders are minors. Don't use the site to contact, profile or target any rider, or to compile information about them.
  • Harming the service. Nothing that overloads, disrupts or damages the site, and no pretending to be PICL Stats or the league.

Accounts

Coach and league accounts are by invitation. Keep your password to yourself, use the account only for your own team or role, and tell us at support@piclstats.com if you think someone else has used it. We may suspend or remove an account that breaks these terms.

Who owns what

Race results belong to the league and race organizers, who publish them on pamtb.org and through their timing provider. Those published results are the official record. The site itself (its statistics, ratings, forecasts, charts, design and code) is the work of Single Track Software, and these terms govern its use.

Accuracy

The site is provided as is. Results are copied from the league's published results, and statistics, ratings and forecasts are estimates computed from them. They can contain mistakes, and a forecast is not a promise. If something looks wrong, please tell us. We aren't liable for decisions made from what the site shows.

Privacy

What the site holds, and how a family can ask for a rider to be hidden, is on the privacy page.

Changes

We may update these terms; the date at the top moves when we do. We may also block access that breaks them, including by automated means.
